SundayPaper

The AI document tool for the church.

Split scanned songbooks into single songs, lay out service programs, make parish magazines, large-print editions and forms — all with professional Typst layout, a PDF engine and OCR under the hood. The print the church makes every week, without fighting Word.

Keep me postedAll products
What it does

Print without typesetting expertise

From a scanned songbook to a finished program — with book quality, automatically.

Songbook split

Split a scanned songbook into single songs with OCR — ready for the catalog in SundaySong.

Service programs

Make beautiful programs with professional Typst typography, not clumsy Word templates.

Parish magazine

Lay out the magazine with columns, images and clean layout — ready for print or PDF.

Large print

Generate large-print editions automatically for those who need bigger text.

Forms

Make sign-up and collection forms that look like the church, not a spreadsheet.

OCR & PDF

pdfium, lopdf and Tesseract turn PDFs into editable, searchable content.

Print, made simple

Professional layout without being a typesetter.

SundayPaper uses Typst — the same class as professional book production — but hides the complexity behind ready-made templates, so a volunteer can make print that looks professional.

  • The Typst engine gives book quality automatically
  • OCR turns scanned pages into editable text
  • From setlist to finished program in one click
