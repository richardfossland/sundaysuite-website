SundayInfo

Your church, on every screen.

Turn any screen in the building into the church's noticeboard. Service times, today's plan, announcements, weather and a verse for the season — composed from your phone or laptop and shown on a TV, a Chromecast, a PC or a Raspberry Pi. Multi-tenant and multi-editor, with a local cache so it keeps running when the network doesn't.

What it does

One screen, always current

Everything a visitor needs to see in the foyer, kept up to date by itself.

Any screen

TV browser, Chromecast, a PC or a Raspberry Pi — if it shows a web page, it shows SundayInfo.

Many editors, many churches

Multi-tenant from the start, with roles so several people can keep the boards up to date.

Knows the church year

Advent, Lent, Easter and ordinary time — the look and the verse follow the season automatically.

Pairs in seconds

A code on the TV, a tap from your phone — the screen is claimed once and never asks again.

Survives a network drop

A local snapshot keeps the last content on screen through an outage, then catches up on its own.

Vipps QR & live data

Show a Vipps giving QR, the weather and live data alongside the plan, refreshed on the screen's own clock.

Built for the foyer

Always on, always current — even when the network isn't.

A 30-second heartbeat is the source of truth, realtime is just a hint, and a local snapshot survives a dropped connection. The screen runs on its own clock, so the church year and the day's mode are always right.

  • Multi-tenant with roles from day one
  • Pairs once; only a hashed token is stored
  • Keeps showing content through a network outage
