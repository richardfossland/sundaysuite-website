SundayWelcome

A digital welcome card for first-time visitors, so the church can follow up.

Usable, but not a priority at the moment.

The visitor scans a QR code, for example on a card in the pew or on a screen, and fills in a short form on their own phone. The church team sees the answers and can get in touch.

The details belong to the church. See the privacy policy.
