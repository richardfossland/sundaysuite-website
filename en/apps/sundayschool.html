SundaySchool

A music and theology school for the church, with lessons in eleven subjects.

The subjects include piano, guitar, bass, drums, leading worship, sight-reading, rhythm, sound engineering and theology.

The school has a library of 51 hymns in 104 arrangements that can be played back. The rights to them have been cleared.
