The philosophy

The first church-technology ecosystem built for Nordic reality.

The world's best church tools are built for American churches. Sunday Suite starts with the Norwegian and Nordic reality — TONO, Bokmål and Nynorsk, privacy and local control — and has global ambitions from there.

TONO as a first-class citizen

TONO is part of the data model from the first row: every work can carry a TONO ID, and every use can note whether it was streamed (a separate royalty pool). That's the Nordic ambition, not an afterthought.

Local and private first

Recording, video and transcription run on your own machine. Your data stays with you unless you choose to share it.

One account, all Sunday

The goal is one sign-in across Rec, Stage, Plan and Song, with keys kept safely in the keychain.

Better together

When the tools talk to each other

The real magic happens at the seams. This is how the Sunday apps are designed to play together as they're finished.

Stage→Rec cue becomes a chapter marker
Stage→Rec lyrics become SRT captions
Plan→Stage setlist becomes a published service
Rec→Plan transcript returns as metadata
Stage→Song every shown song can be logged for TONO/CCLI
Plan→Paper setlist becomes a printed program in one click
Paper→Song a scanned songbook becomes catalog entries
Rec→Paper the sermon becomes a parish-magazine draft
Rec→Edit sermon + transcript ready for captioning
