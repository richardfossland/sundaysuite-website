The tools

Gather a room in one click

Each one runs live in the browser — open it on a shared screen and everyone joins from a phone. Click a card to start.

Icebreaker

SundayQuiz

Get-to-know-you bingo for a first gathering — everyone hunts for people who match the squares, and the room warms up fast.

Any group · 5 minOpen
Classroom

SundayChess

A big-screen chess tournament for the classroom — Swiss rounds and a knockout, run from one screen with a solo bot to practice against.

Classroom · solo or teamsOpen
Sport & play

SundayTurnering

A live tournament board for any sport or game — leagues, cups and playoffs, with a big-screen view and a phone in every hand.

Any sport · league or cupOpen
Group game

SundayMarket

A fast, friendly trading game for a group — buy low, sell high, dodge the famine and out-trade the table before the bell.

Group · fast roundsOpen
Party game

SundayHarvest

Biblical social deduction — wheat among the tares (Matthew 13). No one gets eliminated; everyone plays to the final reveal.

Party · no eliminationOpen
Classroom

SundayTicTacToe

Tic-tac-toe as a tournament — three board sizes, Swiss rounds and a knockout, run from one screen with a bot to practise against.

Classroom · 3×3 to 5×5Open
Fundraiser

SundayBasar

A digital church bazaar — sell raffle tickets and draw the prizes live on the big screen. The app never touches money; you confirm each Vipps payment yourself.

Any event · live drawOpen
Youth

SundayPanel

Anonymous questions from the floor to a panel on the big screen — no app, no login. Everyone asks with a word-code or QR; you curate what goes up.
